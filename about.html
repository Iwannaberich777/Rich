My Story

This isn't a collection notice. It's an honest statement.

I stepped out of the Matrix a while ago. I see myself standing on top of a green mountain, looking down at society — at everyone, like characters in a video game, playing out boring roles, in boring, underpaid jobs.

I'm no longer interested in what used to interest me. My mind is on a whole different level now. I have ideas, I have projects. But I'm missing something material: money.

I was born into a poor family, in a country devastated by corruption. I never had a single real opportunity at anything.

My dream is to have even just $1,000M USD — which to you means almost nothing, but to me would be my entire life. I'd be in debt for it, in this life and the next.

If anyone reading this feels the urge to do some good in this life, I'd be grateful to receive it.
